About the group

Four decades of precision, held together by one family of firms.

Since 1981, the Khandelwal Busar Group has grown from a single Faridabad press shop into three specialised firms — each with its own plant, certifications and client book, yet drawing on the same discipline of finishing to a tolerance rather than a target.

Together we span two states and the full supply chain for metal components: the raw cold-rolled strip, the drawn wire and armour, and the pressed, welded assemblies that reach the production lines of India's largest OEMs.
