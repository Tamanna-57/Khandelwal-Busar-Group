    About the group

    One family of companies covering the whole metal supply chain.

    Khandelwal Busar Industries started in Faridabad in 1981 as a press shop supplying Maruti Suzuki. It now makes axles, chassis, cabins and brackets for many of India's vehicle makers, and its Alwar unit produces the galvanised tape and wire that armour power and telecom cables.

    Metal Coatings (India) Ltd, promoted by the Khandelwal family in 1994 and listed on the BSE, cold rolls steel strip on the plot next door. Each company has its own plant, certifications and customers.
