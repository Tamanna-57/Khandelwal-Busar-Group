The Group

Founded in Faridabad in 1981, the Khandelwal Busar Group has grown into three specialist manufacturers that cover the whole metal supply chain: the strip, the wire and the finished assembly.

Khandelwal Busar Industries started as a press shop supplying Maruti Suzuki. It now makes chassis, axles, cabins and brackets for many of India's vehicle makers. Its Alwar unit produces the galvanised tape and wire that armour the country's power and telecom cables.

Metal Coatings (India) Ltd was promoted by the Khandelwal family in 1994 and is listed on the BSE. It cold rolls steel strip next door at Sector 59. Every company runs its own plant and certifications, and all of them work to the same discipline.
